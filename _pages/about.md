Giuseppe Chindemi is a postdoctoral researcher at the [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch/), mentored by [Benjamin Grewe](https://grewelab.org/biography/) and [Richard Hahnloser](https://www.ini.uzh.ch/en/institute/people?uname=rich) (ETH/UZH) and [Giorgia Ramponi](https://sites.google.com/view/alpi-lab/giorgia-ramponi) (UZH). His research focuses on learning algorithms in biological and artificial neural networks, currently exploring hierarchical control and planning in autonomous agents.

He completed his PhD at the [Blue Brain Project](https://www.epfl.ch/research/domains/bluebrain/) under [Henry Markram](https://people.epfl.ch/henry.markram) (EPFL) and [Eilif Muller](https://mila.quebec/en/directory/eilif-b-muller) (UdeM/Mila), investigating synaptic plasticity through large-scale simulations of neocortical circuits on high-performance computing systems (Mira, ALCF; Blue Brain 5, CSCS). Prior to joining the ETH AI Center, he was a postdoc in the lab of [Camilla Bellone](https://www.unige.ch/medecine/en/faculty/decanat/camilla-bellone) (UNIGE), where he developed [LISBET](https://github.com/BelloneLab/lisbet), an open-source toolkit for discovering social behaviors in pose-tracking data.

**Research interests:** reinforcement learning, hierarchical control, multi-agent systems, planning, computational neuroscience
